        How this is calculated

        1 · Monthly volume

        Your target output, capped at what one Typhoon realistically does in a single shift.

        2 · The drum setup that matches it

        A same-size drum reaches the same monthly output only by running more hours. Holding normal weekly hours, it has to be roughly twice the size, or more than one machine.

        3 · Labour

        Drum roasting hours minus Typhoon roasting hours, at an hourly rate.

        4 · Energy

        Typhoon uses 0.30 kWh/kg against a 0.75 kWh/kg drum baseline. The difference, at your electricity price.

        5 · Defects

        1% defects on a Typhoon against 6% on a drum. Those five points, at green coffee cost.
